Method notes

Numerical paths. The log-price process uses 50 forward-Euler intervals over T = 1. The controller uses the same Brownian increment and the Ito return increment Δr = ΔX + σ²Δt/2. This is not the sampled simple return exp(ΔX) − 1.

What the lines mean. The 100 path traces and their sample mean use the Euler simulation. The expectation curve is the paper’s continuous-time analytical result; finite-sample noise and Euler discretization mean they need not coincide.

Threshold. When shown, K* is Theorem 3’s sufficient bound for positive continuous-time expected gain when K < K*. It is not an optimum, a pathwise guarantee, or a guarantee about the Euler sample mean.
